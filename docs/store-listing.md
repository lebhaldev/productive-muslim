## Full description (max 4000)
Nurday is a calm, private place for your day as a Muslim.

Each day
• One ayah in Arabic with its translation, and the published tafsir (Al-Muyassar and Al-Mukhtasar) when you open it
• One hadith from Sahih al-Bukhari or Sahih Muslim, in Arabic and English, with its reference
• One Arabic quote from classical scholars and poets
• Tap ↻ to see another one

Your rhythm
• Habits with streaks and gentle check-off animations
• How you feel today, in one tap
• A short journal entry for each day, saved as you type
• Activities you did, grouped by day
• A calendar that shows your habits, mood and journal on every day
• Reflect: your last 30 days at a glance

Prayer and weather
• Prayer times calculated on your phone, 12 calculation methods, Standard or Hanafi Asr
• Next-prayer countdown, Qibla direction and the Hijri date
• Local weather for your city

Private by design
• No account and no Nurday server. Your journal, mood and habits stay on your phone
• Optional journal lock with your fingerprint, face or screen lock
• Optional backup: export to a file, or a daily backup to your own Google Drive
• No ads, no analytics, no tracking

Made with care
• Nine colour themes, light and dark, including true black for OLED
• Works offline after the first day
• Every ayah, hadith, tafsir and quote is copied from a named source and never written or changed by the app
